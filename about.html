What this is

Plasma Agent Platform is a design service and software for teams that have to build a plasma process tool without prior data — a new process, a new chamber, a first product. You give the process target. We return the tool design — plasma source, RF matching, operating window — Every value is a prediction before installation, traced to its source; confirmation happens on your tool. We call this path TwinPath.

The engine (Plasma Agent) computes the ion and radical flux at the wafer from coil, electrode or tube geometry and operating conditions in 0.2 s. Plasma Agency is the design service built on it. We take three kinds of request: plasma source, RF matching, and process.

Where we are

What exists — the physics engine (release v3.10, with RF matching), the TwinPath procedure run end to end on a first case with a virtual customer, stated error bands, a Korean patent filing (10-2026-0194058, 2026-10-08) and trademark filings (Plasma Agent · Plasma Agency).

What does not exist yet — a facility of our own, and a first real customer. That first customer is what we are working toward.

What we are looking for — one team starting a new source or process, to take the first steps together: source and matcher first, where agreement shows the day the plasma is lit. Send us your target; we first tell you whether it is in range and computable.

How we work

Physics first, limits first. Every value in a proposal is a prediction before installation, and every number traces back to its source.

You need no data to start, and we do not ask for your raw data. Confirmation happens on your tool, and what you measure stays with you. Agreement with literature and with other tools is reference only — confirmation happens on your tool.

We do ask for your technical feedback. At each step you tell us how it matched — in range or out, and in which direction. That feedback goes back into the part of TwinPath it belongs to, and we tell you where it landed.

"Agent" is a name, not a promise. The design calculations run on physics models and a referenced database; AI is used for literature research and independent review. Every customer document passes an independent AI review and an engineer's final check. Nothing acts on a tool without approval.
