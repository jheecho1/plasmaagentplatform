What this is

Plasma Agent Platform is software and a design service for those who build plasma process tools and those who buy them: source design, process prediction and calibration on one physics engine, before anything is built. The engine (Plasma Agent) computes the ion and radical flux at the wafer from coil, electrode or tube geometry and operating conditions in 0.2 s; the twin (Plasma Agency) fits to measurement and recommends outcomes.

The same engine serves three uses — R&D Agent (now), Process Optimization Agent (next), Machine Control Agent (last). The vision is three; the execution is one at a time.

Who

Built and run by one researcher who has designed and measured plasma sources. The equations, the validation, the design judgement and the responsibility for every result rest with Jeonghee Jo.

Built alone, but not worked alone: an AI research partner (Amy) has been part of the whole process — documents, code drafts, validation design, business planning. That is how a company this small could build this much. The design, the equations, the validation and the responsibility are human.

Where we are

What exists — the physics engine (release frozen), twin v0.5, a plasma-treatment (nitridation) process model validated on 27 in-house points, stated validation ratios and applicability grades, a Korean patent filing (10-2026-0194058, 2026-10-08) and trademark filings (Plasma Agent · Plasma Agency).

What does not exist yet — a facility of our own, and a source designed by the twin that has been built and verified. So the current path is software + design service + validation on external tools (a pilot tool or a public fab). A physical reference source comes after funding.

What we are looking for — one pilot to apply the design service on its next source, and RF component makers and labs who will try the engine first. Send us your spec; we first tell you whether it is in range and computable.

How we work

Physics first. Every number can be traced to an equation, a data source and a calibration record, and the limits are written down rather than hidden (trends ±20 %, absolute density ×0.5–1.2). The twin does not control the tool — it reads, recommends and waits for approval. That is the rule today; tool control is the last stage of the roadmap, done together with tool makers.

"Agent" is a name, not a promise. Today the engine assists; the next stage recommends; only the last stage acts, within an approved envelope. One engine, three levels of autonomy.
